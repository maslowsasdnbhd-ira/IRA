* **Project Name:** MASLOW SA Sdn Bhd Corporate & Training Platform

## 3. Selected Website Design Idea: "The Sanctuary of Growth" (Idea #2)
* **Design Concept:** A clean, spacious, empathetic aesthetic designed to immediately lower anxiety and build trust. It utilizes gentle card layouts, calming warm tones, clear typography, and a prominent "Teacher Support & Mental Health Training" value proposition above the fold.
* **Key Layout Sections:**
  1. **Navigation Bar:** Logo (MASLOW SA), Quick Links (About, Programs, 20+ Years Expertise, Contact), and a "Get in Touch" CTA button.
  2. **Hero Section:** Empathetic headline addressing international school teacher burnout, supported by a clear subheading about 20+ years of expert training, with dual CTAs (Explore Programs & Book Consultation).
  3. **The Struggle & Solution Section:** A 3-column empathy grid highlighting teacher stress points in international schools and how MASLOW SA's specialized training brings balance and resilience.
  4. **Our 20+ Years Legacy / Credibility Bar:** Statistics and trust badges highlighting two decades of training excellence.
  5. **Training Modules Preview:** Cards detailing specific mental health and resilience workshops.
  6. **Interactive Contact & Consultation Form (Primary CTA):** Name, school name, email, role, and message fields with success state validation.
  7. **Footer:** Copyright, social links, and accreditation info.
